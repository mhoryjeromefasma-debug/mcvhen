About

Hi, I’m Mc Vhen.

I’m Mc Vhen Asma, an SEO specialist from Misamis Oriental, Philippines, with 1 year of hands-on SEO experience on live client websites. I help businesses reach customers who are already searching for them, on Google, in Maps, and in AI tools like ChatGPT. I studied Management at Mindanao State University, then moved into SEO at Run Digital.

Most of my days went to keyword research, site audits, on page and technical fixes, local listings, and checking results in Search Console and Analytics. I like work I can measure and explain in plain words.

My work is white hat and data driven. I don’t promise #1 rankings. I promise a clear plan, plain language reports, and consistent work you can measure.
